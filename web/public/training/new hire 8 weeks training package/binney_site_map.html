UPark Site MapBuildings, access and BMS — blank — you fill in the buildings

The streets are given. The buildings are not.

Walk the site and write each building into its block on the map, then fill in the table. Seventeen blocks, numbered — the numbers on the map match the rows below.

Getting them wrong the first time is the point. Do it from memory, then walk it and correct yourself.

How to use this oneDo it twice. First from memory at a desk — you will get some wrong, and the ones you get wrong are the ones worth learning. Then walk the site with it and correct yourself in a different colour pen.

Clues are already on the page: street names, which side of Sidney Street a block sits on, and whether it is a garage (dashed) or a building (solid). Massachusetts Avenue is north, Pacific Street is south.
