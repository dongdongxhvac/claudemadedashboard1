These answers are made up. The vendor, the navigation, the point names and every value on this sheet are an illustration only — your site will not match them, and it is not supposed to. Do not copy anything from this page onto your own sheet.

What to copy is the level of detail: point names written exactly as they read, setpoints beside readings, command and status both recorded, and honest answers where something is missing.

What made this a good sheet

Four things, and none of them are about knowing the equipment.

1. Every point name is written exactly as it reads. Not “chilled water temp” but 75SS.CH-1.CHWS-T. After six pages of that, the naming convention becomes obvious — and then the search box replaces clicking through menus.

2. Every reading has its setpoint beside it. 44.2 against 44.0 is a plant doing its job. 44.2 on its own is a number.

3. Command and status are both recorded. Every time. The one place they disagree is the fault you will be sent to find.

4. The gaps are written down honestly. NOT MAPPED for the boiler lockout. A valve that is not being trended. An override nobody could explain. Those three lines are worth more than all the readings — because they are the things the graphic cannot tell you, and now somebody knows.

Note what is not here: no explanation of what a chiller does, no theory, no analysis. Six systems found, eighteen values read, three gaps reported. That is a complete exercise.
