  • The model. Everything below is a record: a dated entry of what was done, where, and how independently. The page rolls those entries up; nothing is a score someone typed in. That's what makes it usable for both career development and job distribution.
  • How independence is shown. Three marks throughout: with lead · solo · solo with no operation interruption. Counts are times done. Knowledge rows use for "walked / can explain".
  • Knowledge = set-up sheet + sign-offs. Each building gets a set-up sheet written by the lead (88 is shown from your description). Find It & Tag It signs off one system at a time against that sheet, and the knowledge cell shows signed / total. Header, OT counts, and COVE completions are real tables today; building records, sheets, and training rows need one new table. Those rows are example.
